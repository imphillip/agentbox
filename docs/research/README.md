# Research notes

Point-in-time technical surveys and working market research from the ongoing agentbox exploration. Unlike the essays, these notes collect evidence and compare concrete implementations, suppliers, and product forms. Capabilities can change quickly, so they should be treated as inputs to experiments rather than purchasing advice or product commitments.

- **[Agent Runtime, Hosting, and Supplier Landscape](agent-runtime-hosting-and-supplier-landscape.md)** - a September 2026 comparison of managed, self-hosted, and integrated agent forms, with Meta Muse as a consumer-product reference and five proof-of-concept architectures.
